Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Brewmap helps you find coffee shops near you in Kyrgyzstan. This page explains what information the app uses and where it goes.

What stays on your device

Your profile details (name, email and password you type when you create a profile), saved coffee shops, reviews, language and light/dark choice are stored in your own browser (local storage). Brewmap has no server of its own and does not receive this information. If you clear your browser data, or use "Delete account" in Settings, it is removed.

Location

If you allow it, your browser shares your location with the app so the map can centre on you and show distances. Your location is not stored by Brewmap. To find nearby coffee shops, the area you are looking at on the map is sent to Google (see below). You can refuse or withdraw location permission in your browser settings; the app then opens on Bishkek.

Google Maps and Places

Maps, coffee shop listings, photos, ratings and opening hours come from Google Maps Platform. When you use them, your browser contacts Google and sends your search text and map area. Google handles this under the Google Privacy Policy.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical information such as your IP address when you visit, under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

What we do not do

We do not currently use advertising, analytics or tracking cookies, and we do not sell or share your information. If we add error tracking or accounts stored on a server, we will update this page first.

Children

Brewmap is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.

Contact

Questions or requests: open an issue at github.com/aiyma-a/brewmap/issues.
